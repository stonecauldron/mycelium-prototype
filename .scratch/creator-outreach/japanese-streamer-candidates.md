## Recommended — 孫守まごも / Magomo Magomori

- **Channel:** [孫守まごも / Magomo Magomori](https://www.youtube.com/@magomo_magomori). **12.7k subscribers** verified directly in the live YouTube channel header on the research date. Japanese-language gaming VTuber represented by Specialite; the [agency profile](https://specialite.games/talents/magomo-magomori/) confirms the channel and official X account.
- **Recent relevant stream:** [Pikmin 2 — searching for Louie alongside treasure](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OuveNyYiSXE), **21 July 2026**, **4:05:40**, **2,994 views**, **242 likes**. Exact date and metrics verified in the expanded public YouTube video description. Japanese title: 【ピクミン2】ルーイが迷子なのでお宝のついでに探します【すぺしゃりて / 新人vtuber 孫守まごも】.
- **Repeated interest:** Direct [YouTube channel search](https://www.youtube.com/@magomo_magomori/search?query=%E3%83%94%E3%82%AF%E3%83%9F%E3%83%B3) shows four public streams: [Pikmin 1](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NQgtfXOo5wI), about 5.7k views and 9h16m; [Pikmin 2 first stream](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Dnmip3bktkk), about 4.4k views and 6h39m; [Pikmin 2 continuation](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3Z6yctsgMjc), about 3.8k views and 5h30m; and the July 21 stream above. The first three dates (July 14, 17, 18, 2026) are corroborated by the [stream index](https://ckworks.jp/vinforadar/vtuber/%E5%AD%AB%E5%AE%88%E3%81%BE%E3%81%94%E3%82%82/), while the July 21 date was checked first-party.
- **Fit assessment (inference):** Repeated recent Pikmin play suggests a plausible audience for Auto Shrooms' small creature squads and growing/training mushroom troops. Lead with creature attachment and squad choices. No direct interest in auto-battlers or Auto Shrooms was verified; the channel primarily emphasizes Splatoon, and the agency lists action shooting as the preferred game category.
- **Contact:** The agency profile's Contact link leads to [REALITY Studios' inquiry form](https://reality-studios.inc/contact/). Name Specialite and 孫守まごも when enquiring. [Official X](https://x.com/magomo_magomori) is also linked by the agency and her video descriptions. No open-DM availability, fees, or willingness to accept an indie game pitch was verified.
