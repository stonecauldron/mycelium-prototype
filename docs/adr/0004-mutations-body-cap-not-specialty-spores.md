# Mutations (body + cap) replace specialty spores for player identity

Player specialty identity used to be sold as distinct shop Spores that each locked a full Strain (art + effect). That blocked combining identities with Lineage and conflated species packages with Nursery gardening. We split identity into **Mutations** — two slots, Body and Cap — separate from **Fertilizers**, removed spores from the Shop, and plant fresh Common grows by paying biomass on a Plot. Lineage spores carry Mutations (and trainings / mean stats / tier), not Fertilizer items. Unit art is layered Generalist body + cap sprites tinted by slot (with fixed empty-slot defaults), then multiplied by Tier; Body owns mount/animation and may adjust scale/hurtbox, not the physics body collider.

We rejected stackable “strain-effect fertilizers” (unbounded chips, muddy Fertilizer meaning) and keeping specialty spore SKUs alongside mutations (two acquisition channels). Shop offer rows stay split (Fertilizer vs Mutation); mutation cost is fertilizer cost + 1; Rotten Thumb discounts pay-on-plot planting; Fertilizer Spreader stays fertilizers-only.
