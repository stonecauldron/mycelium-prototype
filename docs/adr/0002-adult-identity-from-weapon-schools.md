# Adult fighting identity comes from weapon-school Training

Children and Adults use a Cocoon for weapon-school Training; a Child completes Evolution into an Adult, while Adult Training completes instantly on confirmation, leaving Stats and troop position unchanged. The Child action is **Evolve** and the Adult action is **Train**; Evolution names the Child-to-Adult transition, not an additional stage for Adults with Combo weapons. One current school yields that school’s Weapon and two yield a Combo weapon; further Training normally replaces the oldest current school, but replaces the newer one if oldest-first replacement would keep the same Weapon. Adult Training that cannot change the Weapon is unavailable and costs no Biomass. Adults and descendants use the same replacement rule, and Lineage spores inherit the parent's current Trainings. Starter packages may directly author a dual-trained Adult.

Revised after playtests showed players did not discover descendants or understand that they enabled Combo weapons: the original Child-only rule hid weapon progression behind an undiscovered lineage loop. Adult Training makes combos accessible within one unit's life while Child Training retains its Stat-growth role. The two-school limit keeps the combo matrix readable; free equip via a weapon Shop remains a rejected alternative.
