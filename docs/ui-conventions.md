# UI conventions

Use the vocabulary in [CONTEXT.md](../CONTEXT.md). Presentation previews describe an action; authoritative game rules still decide whether it can execute. See [expected action rejections](adr/0014-explain-expected-action-rejections.md).

Plain `tooltip_text` uses the shared theme's compact cream-and-green paper panel with 22px Fredoka text in dark ink, including disabled controls and settings sliders. Use the default tooltip for short text; no per-control script is required. Rich tooltip cards use `DetailTooltipPopup.configure()` so their native hover wrapper stays transparent while Godot owns hover timing and dismissal. Use `configure(null)` for an intentionally empty placeholder without replacing an existing overlay card.

In Training comparisons, emphasize nonzero changes with larger result Attack/HP/attack-speed icons and numerals (130% size), 24px signed changes, and 24px text with proportionally enlarged icons for changed STR/DEX/CON rows. Unchanged values retain their normal size. Reset the emphasis when a refreshed preview no longer changes a value.

In the Training confirmation, Attack, HP, attack speed, Weapon tags and visible STR/DEX/CON rows show short paper descriptions and the shared HoverPunch animation. Describe each side's own Weapon scaling, including the higher of Strength or Dexterity for Finesse. Empty delta rows and the result-only Cocoon drag preview remain passive. Hover feedback must preserve the comparison's change colors, sizes and spacing.

Weapon details show a stopwatch chip followed by **Base Damage: N** on one centered line. The stopwatch displays seconds between attacks, with up to two decimal places and an `s` suffix. Details use the Weapon's authored interval; Training previews include the Unit's persistent attack-rate and Seal bonuses. Put speed after Attack and HP in both comparison and result-only Cocoon previews. A shorter interval is better: show it in gain green with a negative signed time change; a longer interval uses loss red with a positive change. Compare rounded displayed intervals, and keep an unchanged interval neutral with no delta.

Keep the stopwatch as simple as the sword and heart chips: an opaque flat teal face, thick solid black outline, centered hands and one top button. Use a 128 × 128 transparent source image. Place its numbers in the lower part of the face so the hands stay visible, including when the result chip enlarges. Reserve space before the signed delta. Other stat chips keep their centered numerals.

Within each Training combat row, give Attack, HP and speed equal-width slots and center the row. A longer change label widens all three slots equally, rather than pushing the stopwatch farther away. Recompute widths on refresh so shorter labels restore compact spacing.

Fit Training portraits using painted sprite bounds, excluding transparent image padding. Both sides share a fitting multiplier and idle animation phase so changing Weapons does not change an Adult's apparent body size. Preserve authored Child-to-Adult proportions, center the bodies above their combat chips, and keep both held Weapons inside their portrait areas when the dialog resizes.

While dragging a troop Unit over an eligible Cocoon, show a compact passive paper tooltip containing only the resulting Unit: portrait, chronological Training slots, Weapon tags, changed Stats and Attack/HP/attack speed, and evolution duration or **Instant**. The Training confirmation keeps the full before-and-after comparison. Share the result renderer and calculations so both previews agree, including formation-dependent Seal bonuses. Fit the result portrait independently of the hidden current Unit. Keep the tooltip out of pointer and keyboard input, update it when switching Schools, and clear it when leaving, cancelling, dropping, hiding, or pausing. Hovering never changes the Unit or spends Biomass; dropping still opens the confirmation. Unaffordable but eligible training can be previewed; blocked and unchanged training keep their existing rejection feedback.

Training comparisons and Unit detail cards share the same weapon equation: two paper Training slots in chronological order, oldest on the left and newest on the right, separated by `+`, then `=` and the resulting Weapon icon. Label unfilled slots `Empty`. The comparison's before side uses the current Unit; its after side uses the preview Unit, including replaced Trainings. Highlight only the newly appended Training with blue-bordered paper. A retained Training that shifts left keeps its green border. Empty slots, the before side, and Unit detail cards also keep green borders. Resolving the combo Weapon must never reorder the displayed Trainings. Keep Weapon tags below the equation and the result Weapon details available on hover in the comparison.

Reject Adult Training that would leave the ordered Trainings unchanged, such as Bow + Bow trained with Bow again. Explain that there are no changes beside the blocked drop target; if shown in a confirmation preview, keep both slots neutral and show a disabled **No changes** button without a price or Biomass icon. It must neither preview a cost nor spend Biomass. Changing the order of two different Trainings still matters because it changes which one will be replaced next. A Child with the same recipe may still Evolve, so its newly applied Training remains highlighted and the action remains available.

In Training comparisons, keep Weapon tags' rounded shape and tint their fill with the light gain/loss palette: green for added tags on the result side, red for removed tags on the before side. Changed range or scaling shows the old tag in red and the new tag in green. Enlarge changed tags with 20px text (normally 16px), proportionally larger icons and padding. Shared tags keep their neutral appearance and normal size. Compare the tag's gameplay meaning, including scaling Stats, rather than its position or caption.

## Cocoon emergence, hatching and composting

Completed Evolutions reveal once at their Cocoon on returning to the War Chamber, after required choices and the camera pan. The Day summary keeps its existing completion entry. Instant Child Evolution and Adult Training use the same reveal immediately after confirmation; the animation adds no game-day wait. Queue committed completions explicitly rather than deriving events from refreshed slots. Launch the resulting Unit from the Cocoon along an airborne arc to its actual Squad or Bench portrait position, at that portrait’s normal scale throughout. Wait for destination layout and keep its live card hidden but animating during the flight. Hand over to that same portrait at the same position and idle phase without fading, teleporting or rebuilding other cards. Starting and finishing a reveal must preserve unrelated portraits and their animation phases.

Cocoon emergence is cosmetic: keep formation drops, Training, Squad unlocks and Start Battle available. Unrelated actions may continue during the flight. If an action moves or replaces the arriving Unit, reuses its Cocoon, changes the landing layout, or starts Compost or Battle, cancel the transient reveal and let the action proceed immediately. Reusing a School consumes its older queued presentation without changing that completed Unit. Continue any remaining queued reveals after the new layout and dialogs settle. Starting Battle discards pending reveal events; they must never delay combat or replay on the next Base visit.

Harvest snapshots the visible egg before clearing the Plot and animates the actual Units successfully added to the Troop. Hatchlings land at normal scale, then visibly walk right toward the War Chamber until their complete bodies and Weapons are off-screen. Show their existing result cards afterward. Multiple hatch results spread out with staggered motion; never shrink a Unit to fit a group. Overflow that did not enter the Troop is never shown as a reward. Keep all result cards within the viewport.

Use a brief shell anticipation, split shell, warm local flash and sparks, airborne reveal, and soft landing. Dim the screen behind the animation as anticipation builds, then restore it after release; the shell and revealed Units remain bright. Preserve authored life-stage proportions, Mutations, Tier tints and the actual held Weapon. Never fit, squash or stretch Unit bodies during the reveal. Only the shell deforms, and Unit shadows remain on the ground.

After a successful Compost transaction, compress the bin against its planted base, spring it open with dust, and send the actual released lineage Spore icons in arcs off the left edge toward the Nursery. Use the committed Stock additions, including FIFO replacement when Stock is full. Child composting animates the bin without inventing spores. Restore the live bin at its normal pose when the effect finishes or is cancelled.

`UnitEmergence` and `CompostRelease` own presentation, screen dimming and audio only. Economic and lifecycle transactions finish before it starts. Cancel transient visuals and their owned sound on tab hide, Undo, pause, scene exit or viewport resize; cancellation does not repeat or reverse the action. Play the Cocoon cork-pop at shell release using its authored transient offset, with a brief scoped music dip; eggs use the distinct Harvest cue and compost release uses the Compost cue. See [audio conventions](../assets/audio/README.md).

## Battle previews

Each grouped enemy keeps its count beside the portrait and centers its Attack/HP chips beneath that whole count-and-portrait group. Preview chips are 32px with 20px numerals; detail tooltip chips remain 40px with 24px numerals. Both use authored averages; actual Stats roll at Battle launch, and hovering the portrait or chips still opens the detail tooltip.

Hovering any Day marker in the current chapter temporarily previews that Day in Scout, even when another Day is pinned. Leaving the marker restores the pinned Day, or the upcoming Battle when nothing is pinned. Clicking a marker pins its preview so the player can move onto enemy portraits and inspect their tooltips; clicking another marker switches the pinned preview. Keep the selected circle or elite skull highlighted while the actual upcoming-day marker stays in place. Show **Battle: Day N** for normal previews and **Elite Battle: Day N** for elite previews. A paper **Back to today** button replaces Scout's reroll control while pinned; clicking the selected marker again or the actual upcoming Day also returns to the upcoming army. Day markers support keyboard activation too.

Other-Day previews use that Day's initial army, generated deterministically from the Run seed, and cannot be rerolled. This includes earlier Days in the current chapter; they are previews of the initial composition, not a history of paid rerolls. Previewing never changes the cached upcoming enemy army, reroll cost, biomass, or the Battle that Start Battle launches. Hovering the upcoming Day shows that exact cached army and reward while retaining any pinned Day and its return control; reroll stays unavailable while a pin exists. Clicking the upcoming Day clears the pin and restores normal reroll availability without a redundant return control. Leaving the War Chamber or refreshing it for a new Day clears the pinned preview and releases offscreen keyboard focus.

Normal Day markers use one circle: selection tints its fill, and keyboard focus reuses its border rather than adding another ring. Elite markers keep their separate selection halo and focus ring. Focus highlights and focus enlargement appear only during keyboard/controller use; pointer input hides them while retaining the navigation focus and any pinned selection. Completed Days replace the circle and Day number with an unnumbered gold trophy using the Biomass icon’s yellow (#DABA6C), with a small cream underline for selection or keyboard focus. Keep the Day available in its tooltip and accessible name. Update completion markers when the Day advances, including within the same chapter. The upcoming-Day triangle remains independent of hover and selection. Day tooltips close when the pointer leaves, including after a click; keyboard tooltips close when focus leaves or the player resumes pointer input. Closing a tooltip does not clear a pinned battle preview.

## Biomass hover previews

Day Summary and Compost replay committed income with `BiomassGain`: the gold outlined gain drops into place, counts from zero while growing, and sends Biomass icons along upward arcs into the counter. Larger gains release more icons (bounded at 40). Arrival pulses grow the counter, with a landing sound, rising collection pops and a completion sound. The Day Summary counter matches the Base header's position and size, including day-label and debug-mode spacing, and retains the final gain in the summary paper. Clicking the summary outside Continue finishes an active reward immediately and consumes that click, including before the drop begins. Continue always advances on its first activation, cancelling any remaining reward presentation and restoring the actual counter total. Compost launches above the bin at release alongside the existing Spores. Use numeric `biomass_amount` summary data and the actual post-Compost balance difference, including Bank income.

Reward animation never grants or delays Biomass. The counter temporarily replays the already committed increase; a purchase, Undo or another balance change restores the authoritative amount immediately. Continue remains available, and pause, tab changes, resize or scene exit cancel owned effects and sounds. Summary cancellation preserves a static gain label. Keep the completion sound alive for its full tail, even if a frame stalls across the end of counting.

The Base biomass counter normally shows the actual balance. Hovering an affordable action that changes biomass enlarges its paper chip and shows the projected balance: **current balance + signed change**. A small cream paper tab shows the signed change. Leaving the action restores the actual balance and normal chip size. Hovering never spends, grants, reserves, or refunds biomass.

Dragged Shop Fertilizers and Mutations show their offer card while the pointer is inside the Shop panel, and their compact Stock form outside it. Crossing either way produces one brief smoke puff. Preserve the item's name, art, tint and Mutation badge. Switching appearance is cosmetic: the drag remains a Shop purchase until a valid drop commits it. Keep the source card's layout space reserved during pickup, and restore it on cancellation.

For Fertilizer and Mutation drags, a Plot or the Sell button is targeted when either the pointer or the visible item icon overlaps it. The icon's fitted bounds exclude the card's name and empty space. A target directly under the pointer wins; otherwise choose the largest icon overlap, then the nearest target center. Use that same native GUI target for highlights, rejection feedback, Biomass previews and release. Keep Sell limited to its paper button, including its displayed hover scale; never make the whole Shop panel a Sell target. Shop items keep their purchase payload and cannot be sold before purchase. Clear drag visuals and targeting on cancellation, pause, tab changes and scene exit.

If the cost exceeds the available balance, keep the actual balance and show it in light loss red with its dark outline. Replace the signed-change tab with **Not enough biomass** in loss red, wrapping onto two lines to fit the paper. Keep the counter enlarged, omit conditional context, and never show a negative projected total. For example, with 3 biomass and a cost of 8, show **3** in red with the warning. A cost of exactly 3 still previews **0** and **−3** normally. Update immediately if the balance or cost changes while hovered; the existing affordability check still prevents the purchase.

| Change | Presentation |
| --- | --- |
| Gain | Green, with a `+` sign on the change. |
| Loss | Red, with a `−` sign on the change. |
| Zero | Neutral result; omit the change tab. |

Use the same gain/loss palette as Training through `StatDisplay.change_color`: gain `Color(0.12, 0.45, 0.18, 1)` and loss `Color(0.7, 0.15, 0.12, 1)`. Biomass result numerals on green paper use the light variants (gain lightened by 0.65, loss by 0.45). Outlined Attack/HP numerals in Training use stronger variants (gain lightened by 0.35, loss by 0.2). Signed changes use the base ink colors on cream paper. Signs carry meaning independently of color. Color reflects the action's signed change; an unaffordable warning uses loss red, and the actual balance uses light loss red with its dark outline.

### Action coverage

- Preview current costs for Shop purchases, paid rerolls, fresh planting, Plot/Squad unlocks, and Evolve/Train confirmation.
- Preview Compost's complete direct biomass payout, including the unit's Bank balance, and Stock sale value. Do not substitute a generic stage payout for a known unit's outcome.
- Drag targets preview the action for the current dragged item or unit: School training, Compost, Stock sales, and Shop purchases dropped into Stock or onto a Plot. Applying an already owned Stock item does not purchase it again.
- Stock sales use the visible Sell button's paper area, including its hover scale; Fertilizer and Mutation icon overlap counts as well as the pointer. The surrounding Shop panel and offers are not sale targets.
- Start Battle previews only the upcoming army's **Battle reward**, labeled **“On victory”**. It is conditional income, not an immediate grant or a forecast of all biomass effects during battle. Scout's other-Day previews do not change this action's projection. See [Battle rewards](adr/0005-day-scaled-battle-reward.md) and [army budgets](adr/0015-budgeted-enemy-armies.md).

### Integration and lifetime

Bind the actionable Control with `BiomassPreview.bind(control, delta_callable, optional_context_string)`. Resolve prices and outcomes from current authoritative data in the callable, including reroll increases and Seal modifiers. Return `null` when the action or drag is inapplicable; an unaffordable but otherwise applicable action still returns its cost so the counter can show the warning. Child controls for free actions, such as Shop locks, must not inherit a purchase preview.

The counter resolves the hovered Control and its ancestors each frame. It refreshes when the balance, action, price, or drag payload changes, and restores when the source is hidden, freed, exited, or no longer applicable. Keep the preview input-ignoring so it cannot steal hover or block the action. A preview must not alter action validation, confirmation requirements, or transaction behavior.
