# Enemy armies spend a Day budget on authored enemy costs

Choosing headcount before types allowed an expensive type to dominate an army without reducing its size. Each Day now supplies a budget range, each enemy type authors a combat-informed cost and Regular/Strong category, and existing patterns supply approximate headcount shares; generation picks eligible types and finds affordable counts, keeping tuning independent of player stats and matchups.

Days 1–4 use Regular enemies, Elite Days use only Strong enemies (at most one distinct type on Day 5, up to three on Day 10), and Days 6–9 allow at most two distinct Strong types. These pool rules replace more complex Strong-budget quotas and contextual selection weights; small armies may round a pattern's minority type away rather than require an exact ratio.

Scout rerolls vary allocated budgets and bias toward the opposite difficulty while retaining prices and the elite restriction. Battle rewards retain the Day base and ±10% swing, but use the army's actual total cost against authored Day-budget bounds: unused points earn nothing, and tiny rounding differences are no longer expanded to the full reward swing by sampled extremes.

The optional guided Run is an explicit exception to budget-generated compositions: `GuidedRun` authors ten small armies so each encounter supports the available systems. Day 5 contains one Log and the final Day-10 elite also uses Strong enemies. Scout and combat consume the same authored specs, and instance Stats are seeded once per Day and preserved through retries. Ordinary Run generation is unchanged.

Guided rewards are also an exception to the difficulty-based economy: each victory grants the next preparation Day's introductory action costs plus 30%, rounded up, with increased authored grants of 16 on Day 7 and 21 on Days 9 and 10, **in addition to savings**. Battle 9 therefore grants 21 biomass, matching Battle 8, for final-Day preparation despite having no new unlocks. This replaces the original guided reward ranges, which produced too much biomass for the restricted choices. The opening is free and starts at zero. Day-8 Seal selection is free, and final victory has no next-Day grant. Current allowances total 114; the action baskets and Day schedule live in the [guided specification](../../.scratch/onboarding-run/spec.md#guided-biomass-allowances). Existing authored army-budget ranges still describe encounter difficulty, but no longer determine guided payouts. Normal action prices, Compost and other earnings, and ordinary starting biomass and Battle rewards remain unchanged.
