# Adult fighting identity comes from weapon-school Training

Children and Adults can enter a Cocoon for weapon-school Training; a Child emerges as an Adult, while Adult Training leaves Stats unchanged. One current school yields that school’s Weapon and two yield a Combo weapon; further Training replaces the oldest current school using the same mechanism for Adults and descendants, and Lineage spores inherit the parent's current Trainings. Starter packages may directly author a dual-trained Adult.

Revised after playtests showed players did not discover descendants or understand that they enabled Combo weapons: the original Child-only rule hid weapon progression behind an undiscovered lineage loop. Adult Training makes combos accessible within one unit's life while Child Training retains its Stat-growth role. The two-school limit keeps the combo matrix readable; free equip via a weapon Shop remains a rejected alternative.
