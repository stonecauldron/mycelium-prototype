# Adult fighting identity comes from weapon-school Training

An Adult’s Weapon is determined by its weapon-school Trainings: one school yields that school’s Weapon; two yield a Combo weapon (further Training replaces the oldest school). Those Trainings (and thus the Weapon) carry on Lineage spores of the same line, so heirs hatch already carrying the bloodline’s schools. Starter packages and seals assume this dual-school ceiling. We rejected a single-school-forever model (too little build variety) and an open N-school stack (combo matrix and readability explode). Free equip via a weapon Shop was an earlier design; that zone is gone, not a parallel identity system. Training is how Adults get their Weapon.
