# Adult fighting identity comes from weapon-school Training

Only a Child can enter a Cocoon for weapon-school Training; it emerges as an Adult and cannot be trained again. Repeated Training happens across a bloodline: Lineage spores carry the parent’s weapon-school Trainings to a Child descendant, which may add its own school when it becomes an Adult. One current school yields that school’s Weapon and two yield a Combo weapon; when a descendant adds another, the oldest current school is replaced while the bloodline’s accumulated Stat gains remain. Starter packages may directly author a dual-trained Adult. We rejected retraining Adults because Training is the Child-to-Adult transition, and rejected an open N-school Weapon stack because the combo matrix and readability explode. Free equip via a weapon Shop was an earlier design; that zone is gone, not a parallel identity system.
